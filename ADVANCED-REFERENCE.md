# Advanced Editor Reference — Tool & Feature Catalog

> Generic, brand-free reference for Inkception's advanced capabilities.
> Legend: ✅ have · 🔶 buildable client-side · 🧠 needs model/API · ➖ skip

## Tools
- ✅ Move/Select (V) — click to select, click empty to deselect, drag to pan
- ✅ Crop (C) + Smart Crop (subject-aware) · Perspective crop 🔶
- ✅ Zoom (Z) — presets 25–800%, Fit/Fill, ⌘0/⌘1/⌘±
- ✅ Hand (H) pan · ✅ Eyedropper (I) color pick
- ✅ Rect / Ellipse / Line / Text (T) / Brush (B) + font, bold/italic, align,
  tracking, leading, color
- ✅ Manual + Smart crop · ✅ Erase brush (transparent) · ✅ Blur brush ·
  ✅ Magic Eraser (AI inpaint)
- 🔶 Marquee (rect/ellipse) · 🔶 Lasso · 🔶 Magic Wand · 🔶 Clone Stamp ·
  🔶 Healing Brush · 🔶 Red Eye · 🔶 Paint Bucket · 🔶 Gradient ·
  🔶 Pen tool + anchor editing · 🔶 Polygon/Star/Triangle shapes ·
  🔶 Dodge/Burn · 🔶 Smudge · 🔶 Warp (curvature/bow) · 🔶 Liquify

## Filters (pixel pipeline — client-side)
- Distort: Pinch, Twirl, Ripple, ZigZag, Glass, Spherical
- Render: Clouds, Difference Clouds, Fibers
- Stylize: Emboss, Find Edges, Glowing Edges, Solarize
- Sketch: Graphic Pen, Halftone
- Sharpen: More, Edges · Noise: Add, Median, Film Grain · Tilt-shift blur
- 🧠 Generative: true text-to-image / advanced inpainting (needs API key)

## Adjustments
- ✅ 6 sliders (brightness/contrast/saturation/exposure/temp/tint)
- ✅ Auto Enhance · ✅ Color Grade / LUT · ✅ Color Palette
- ✅ Curves · ✅ Levels · 🔶 Hue/Sat, Vibrance, Black & White, Gradient Map
- ✅ Quick Actions (20 one-click)

## Generative AI (on-device)
- ✅ Remove / Replace Background (MediaPipe matting) · ✅ Magic Eraser /
  Generative Fill (diffusion inpaint) · ✅ Decompose to layers ·
  ✅ Upscale 2×/4×/8× · ✅ Vectorize · ✅ Retouch · ✅ Denoise ·
  ✅ Smart Crop · ✅ Smart Text Color · ✅ Smart Suggestions

## Layers & Export
- ✅ Layers (opacity, 12 blend modes, dup/delete/lock, AI layers)
- ✅ Collage (12 layouts, Fit/Fill/Rotate/Swap) · ✅ Global search
- ✅ Export 8 formats × 27 platform presets (PNG/JPG/WebP/GIF/MP4/PDF/PSD/SVG)
- ✅ Offline standalone single-file

## Skip
3D modeling, Camera Raw, real-time collaboration, print CMYK, legacy web
slices — not meaningful in a browser editor.
