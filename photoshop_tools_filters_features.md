## 2. Filters

- **Artistic**: Cutout, Dry Brush, Film Grain, Fresco, Neon Glow, Oil Paint,
  Posterize, Rough Pastels, Smudge Stick, Sponge, Underpainting, Watercolor
- **Blur**: Blur, Gaussian Blur, Motion Blur, Radial Blur, Smart Blur, Surface
  Blur, Zoom Blur
- **Brush Stroke**: Accented Edges, Angled Strokes, Crosshatch, Dark Strokes,
  Defocused Circles, Sprayed Strokes, Sumi-e, Thick Paint, White Smoke
- **Distort**: Diffuse Glow, Glass, Ocean Ripple, Pinch, Ripple, Twirl, ZigZag
- **Noise**: Add Noise, Dust & Scratches, Median
- **Pixelate**: Color Halftone, Crystallize, Facet, Fragment, Mezzotint,
  Mosaic, Pointillize
- **Render**: Clouds, Difference Clouds, Fiber, Lens Flare, Lighting Effects
- **Sharpen**: Sharpen, Sharpen Edges, Sharpen More, Smart Sharpen
- **Sketch**: Chalk & Charcoal, Graphic Pen, Halftone, Note Paper, Stamp,
  Torn Edges, Watercolor Wash…
- **Stylize**: Diffuse, Emboss, Extrude, Glowing Edges, Find Edges, Neon Glow,
  Solarize, Trace Contour
- **Texture**: Texturizer, Wave, Wind
- **Video**: Old Video, Pixelize, Shift Channels
- **Other**: Adaptive Wide Angle, Camera Raw, Lens Correction, Liquify,
  Vanishing Point, Blur Gallery (tilt-shift, path blur)
